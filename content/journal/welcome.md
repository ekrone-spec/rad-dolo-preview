My friends call me Rad, and you should too.

I spent years working in fashion in Los Angeles, then brought it all home to Minneapolis. These days you'll find me in other people's closets, and I mean that as the highest compliment. The best style advice never came from a runway. It comes from someone standing next to you at the mirror, telling you the truth.

## What you'll find here

Short notes on getting dressed. How I edit a closet. How I pack one suitcase for ten days. What I tell clients in the fitting room when something almost works. Each note takes about two minutes, because you have places to be.

New notes land here first. If you'd rather have them find you, there's a signup just below.

## Start anywhere

If your closet is full and you still have nothing to wear, start with [Keep, donate, maybe](https://www.raddolo.com/journal/keep-donate-maybe/). If mornings are the enemy, read [Why getting dressed feels exhausting](https://www.raddolo.com/journal/why-getting-dressed-feels-exhausting/). And if you want me in your corner for real, my calendar is one click away.

Your energy introduces you before you ever say a word. Let's make sure it says the right one.

xo, Rad
